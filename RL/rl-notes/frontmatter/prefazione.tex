\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
These notes were written during the \emph{Autonomous and Mobile Robotics}
course held by Prof.\ Giuseppe Oriolo at Sapienza University of Rome.
The main purpose of this document is to provide a coherent and compact
reference for the topics covered: kinematics of mobile robots, motion
planning, motion control, localization, mapping, SLAM, and decision
making under uncertainty. Theoretical derivations are interspersed with
practical remarks, formulas, and, where useful, pseudocode or short
implementations.
